# Jarsag — estudio individual 01

**Fecha:** 2026-10-28 · **Estado vigente:** diseño visual de Jarsag aprobado por el autor.

**Aprobación directa, 28/10/2026:** «muy buena!! me encantó la nueva dirección de Jarsag, queda!». La lámina individual 01 queda como diseño visual vigente: rostro, porte, poncho azul, vestuario, gafas y equipo representado. Las gafas y el libro conservan el carácter opcional establecido en su perfil; la aprobación no los vuelve obligatorios en toda escena. Los esbozos arquitectónicos de fondo siguen siendo ambientación, sin fijar edificios o geografía. El encargo, prompt y observación inicial de abajo se conservan como registro previo a esta aprobación.

Se relee el perfil activo de Jarsag y el control visual de PNJ. Se mantienen 1,80 m, pelaje castaño, ojos oscuros, cuernos y gafas de la referencia específica, poncho azul y doble lectura de alcalde/explorador. Se elige representar las gafas opcionales y un libro de cuero; sin cayado. Una figura principal de cuerpo entero, vista posterior, retrato y detalles materiales permiten revisar el diseño sin la compresión de la lámina grupal.

**Herramienta:** imagegen integrada, modo stylized-concept; una lámina nueva basada en cuatro referencias locales. Originales conservados.

**Referencias:** lámina PNJ del 9/10 (solo Jarsag: vestuario/acabado), recorte específico de cuernos/gafas, `nuevos personajes (11).jpg` y `(26).jpg` (porte y anatomía facial, sin armadura, armas ni pelaje blanco).

**Resultado:** [Lámina individual 01](./jarsag-estudio-conceptual-01.png). Inspección visual: conserva pelaje castaño, ojos oscuros, gafas elevadas, cuernos curvados, poncho azul, libro y pezuñas; sin cayado. Figura completa y vista de espalda legibles. El tejido exacto, cierres, corte de prendas, bolso y síntesis del rostro son propuestas visuales. Las arquitecturas esbozadas de fondo son ambientación, no planos ni edificios aprobados. No se actualiza el perfil canónico por la generación.

## Prompt utilizado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished individual character concept art study sheet for JARSAG, mayor of Romanance in the original epic fantasy world Lumenor. Landscape sheet about 3:2, high resolution, carefully arranged readable studies, generous margins. This is a proposed design for author review, not an illustration of a new story event.
Reference images supplied in order:
1 the old ensemble Lumenor sheet: use ONLY Jarsag in the upper left for the approved teal blue poncho, layered practical cream clothes, leather travel gear, woven trim and painterly illustrative craft. Correct its compressed anatomy; ignore all the other characters and all old labels.
2 Jarsag head crop: primary guide for horns and round aviator goggles on his forehead, nestled naturally between horn bases; goggles lifted like a headband, NOT covering his eyes. Ignore the old text.
3 and 4 two white minotaurs: use only dignified powerful posture and believable bovine facial anatomy, NOT their fur color, armor, skulls, staff, weapons or aggressive mood.
Subject invariants: ONE male mature experienced minotaur, 1.80 metres tall, athletic substantial explorer build with balanced long adult limbs, dignified upright posture. Not squat or giant, no compressed legs or oversized head. Entire fur warm chestnut brown, medium light chestnut with slightly darker muzzle/neck, never white or gray. Dark brown eyes. Recognizably bovine face, intelligent and approachable, quiet confidence and a subtle lively alertness, not a snarling monster. A pair of thick horns extending sideways then curving upwards, like reference2. Same identity in every study.
Clothing: preserve and develop the accepted rich teal/blue woven poncho with restrained original cream/ochre geometric borders, asymmetrical drape opening one forearm for movement; natural cloth cream tunic, comfortable dark earth-colored travel trousers, robust brown leather satchel and practical belt, modest hardware, tidy repaired and lived-in materials. Civic dignity through tailoring and posture combined with genuine readiness to explore. No official invented insignia, no crowns, no medals. Clothing fits a minotaur and joints articulate naturally. Retain natural dark cloven hooves from original design, workable trouser openings, no human shoes pasted over hooves. One small closed brown leather-bound book in a hand, the other hand relaxed; a few folded documents as secondary detail. No staff, no cane, no weapons, no plate armor, no skulls.
Layout: dominant full-length front three-quarter standing figure at left taking about half the sheet, clearly visible full horns to hooves with breathing room. At right a smaller rear three-quarter FULL BODY study showing the actual same poncho construction and satchel, one large expressive head portrait clearly displaying chestnut fur, dark eyes, goggles and horns, plus two small material/prop studies (woven poncho border and leather-bound book/goggles). Supporting drawings lighter and less saturated than main figure. Never stretch a figure to fit the panel. Consistent anatomy, clothing and accessories across all views, no extraneous characters. Sparse delicate graphite construction strokes with confident painted forms, tactile woven cloth and soft fur, crisp focal detail with expressive hand painted edges, sophisticated fantasy concept art, full natural rich color on a quiet warm ivory paper background. Not all sepia, not a photoreal 3D render, not a videogame inventory UI. No scattered white speckle effects, no magic glows or foreign copied glyphs. Minimal tasteful editorial text only: "JARSAG" and beneath it "Alcalde de Romanance · Explorador", a small "1,80 m" and "Estudio conceptual · 01". No invented biographical text.
```
